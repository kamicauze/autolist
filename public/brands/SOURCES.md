# Vehicle brand logo sources

Downloaded October 28, 2026 for vehicle-make identification in Autolist. All marks remain the property of their respective brand owners; no endorsement or affiliation is implied.

55 assets come from [VehicleSpecs brand-logos](https://github.com/vehiclespecs/brand-logos) pinned at commit `3f0929e70e0a9a2a502063edc4b3e5c0146cba74`. Its curation/metadata license and trademark notice are retained in [SOURCE-LICENSE.txt](SOURCE-LICENSE.txt). The source manifest uses ChangAn, Rolls-Royce and Seat for Autolist catalog names Changan, Rolls Royce and SEAT.

The existing modern Kia mark is retained rather than replacing it with the older oval variant. Volvo uses the authentic flat iron mark from Simple Icons pinned at `90646e4bf7f3b89df6c37e65baf3e7bc759a517c`; its CC0 notice is retained in [SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.md](SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.md). These assets identify the makes; this is not a claim that every mark is the newest brand identity.

GWM uses the current mark downloaded directly from the [official GWM global homepage](https://www.gwm-global.com/), which references the image below.
